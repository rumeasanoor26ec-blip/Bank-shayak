Safe Digital Payment Walkthrough

Follow these 4 simple steps to pay safely using UPI or Mobile Banking.

1
Open Your Trusted Bank / UPI App

Unlock your phone and open BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, or your official bank app.

2
Scan QR or Enter Verified Mobile Number

Tap "Scan QR" at the shop counter and verify the receiver's registered banking name on screen.

3
Enter Amount & Check Carefully

Type the exact rupee amount. Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

4
Enter Secret 4 or 6-Digit UPI PIN

Cover your screen while typing your PIN. Wait for the green checkmark confirmation.

⚠️ Anti-Fraud Rule: Bank staff will NEVER ask for your OTP, ATM PIN, or UPI PIN over phone or at the counter.
